Privacy Notice

How InfinAI handles your data — plainly explained.

Last updated: 5 May 2026

Who we are

InfinAI is an AI consultancy helping small and medium businesses adopt AI in a way that actually works for them. This privacy notice covers how we handle personal data on infinai.uk, including information you share with our AI assistant, Buddy.

If you have any questions about this notice or how we handle your data, contact us at support@infinai.uk.

What we collect

When you interact with Buddy on our site, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address and/or phone number
  • Your business name and your role
  • The content of your conversation with Buddy
  • A session ID and timestamp (used internally to keep the conversation working)

We only collect what you choose to share. You're not required to give us any of this to browse the site.

We do not collect special category data (health, political views, etc.), and we do not use cookies for tracking or advertising on this site.

Why we collect it

We collect this information for one reason: so we can follow up with you about your interest in InfinAI's services. That's it. We don't use it for anything else.

Our legal basis

Under UK GDPR, we rely on legitimate interest as our legal basis for processing this data. Specifically: when you start a conversation with Buddy and share your details, you're indicating an interest in our services, and we have a legitimate interest in following up with you about that.

If you'd rather we didn't, you can ask us to delete your data at any time (see "Your rights" below).

Where it's stored

Your data is stored in Supabase, hosted on AWS infrastructure within the European Union. It is not transferred outside the EU.

Who we share it with

Nobody. We don't sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing or any other purpose.

The only parties who handle your data on our behalf are our infrastructure providers (Supabase and AWS), and they do so under contract, only for the purpose of running our services.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and conversation data for 90 days from your last contact with us, after which it's automatically deleted. This applies whether or not you go on to become a customer.

Separately, if you do become a customer, we keep business records — such as contracts and invoices — for as long as we have an active relationship with you, plus any period required for legal or accounting reasons.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you
  • Correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Object to how we're processing it
  • Withdraw consent or ask us to stop processing at any time
  • Complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) if you believe we've handled your data badly — ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these rights, email support@infinai.uk. We'll respond within one month.

Changes to this notice

If we update this notice, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top. Material changes will be flagged on the site.
