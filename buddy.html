What Buddy does

Designed to handle the questions your team sees every day

Buddy supports Telegram-based support workflows by taking care of repetitive conversations that would otherwise consume time and delay responses.

Instant responses

Answers common support questions as soon as they arrive, helping users get support without waiting for an admin to respond.

Guided troubleshooting

Walks users through clear steps for issues like login problems, access requests, connection errors, and account questions.

Human escalation

When a case is unusual, unresolved, or sensitive, Buddy passes it to your team instead of forcing a weak automated response.
