What Buddy Does

  • Answers common support questions instantly
  • Guides users through troubleshooting steps
  • Escalates complex issues to human support
  • Maintains conversation context
  • Provides 24/7 support availability

How It Works

Users message your Telegram support channel as usual.

Buddy recognises common issues and responds with helpful guidance. If a problem cannot be resolved automatically, the issue is escalated to a human team member.
