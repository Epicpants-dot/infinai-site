The Problem

Many businesses and online communities receive the same support questions again and again. Login problems, account access issues, status requests, and basic troubleshooting requests.

Answering these messages repeatedly slows response times and consumes valuable team resources.

The InfinAI Approach

InfinAI builds practical AI systems that automate repetitive conversations while ensuring complex issues are escalated to a human when needed.

This allows businesses to respond instantly to common requests while maintaining control over complex support cases.

Buddy Support Bot

Buddy is an AI assistant designed to manage support conversations inside Telegram.

It recognises common support questions, provides helpful guidance, and escalates complex issues to your team when needed.

Typical deployments automatically handle 60–85% of repetitive support messages.
